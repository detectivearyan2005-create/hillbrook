Add some adventure
to your stay

Bring an adventurous spirit to your Hillbrook experience. From thrilling water activities to scenic trail explorations, we can arrange everything for you. Let us help you create unforgettable memories with exciting adventures tailored to your stay.

Guided Island tours

Discover the beauty of our local islands by paddleboard, kayak, or small boat. Let our expert guide lead you on an unforgettable adventure through crystal-clear waters to one of the Coromandel's hidden gems.

E-biking

Choose from a range of e-bikes and mountain bikes, available to hire directly from Hillbrook. Just 5 minutes away, enjoy access to one of the region's top mountain bike parks — or take a relaxed ride around the farm roads.

Surf lessons

Learn to surf on the stunning beaches near Whangamata, guided by expert instructors. Perfect for all skill levels, this exciting activity offers an unforgettable New Zealand adventure.

Personalised Day Tours

Let us curate a day to remember. In partnership with Kiwi Dundee Adventures, we offer fully personalised tours tailored to your interests — whether it's Māori culture, scenic hikes, hidden gems off the beaten track, or simply a relaxed day of sightseeing and local flavour. From immersive rainforest hikes among ancient kauri trees and volcanic peaks, to leisurely explorations of the Coromandel's most iconic landscapes, each experience is designed just for you.

https://kiwidundee.co.nz/

Glass Bottom Boat

Explore Te Whanganui o Hei Marine Reserve above and below the surface. Relax aboard their Glass Bottom Boat, view the fish and marine life in their natural habitat. Soak up the stunning scenery above the water with highlights Cathedral & Orua Sea Cave or take the plunge and snorkel amongst the schools of fish!

www.gbbw.co.nz

The Lost Spring

Relax in the Geo-Thermal Pools, be pampered in the tree top Day Spa or dine in their Restaurant, there are so many options at The Lost Spring, Whitianga.

www.thelostspring.co.nz

Hahei explorer

Cruise the Coromandel's stunning coastline and discover the iconic Cathedral Cove with Hahei Explorer. Glide through crystal-clear waters, past dramatic cliffs and sea caves, soaking in the coastal serenity.

www.haheiexplorer.co.nz

Coromandel Ocean Adventures

Experience an unforgettable marine adventure to the Aldermen Islands, one of Aotearoa's top destinations for diving and wildlife viewing. Enjoy crystal-clear waters, vibrant marine life, and included snorkelling gear. There are also daily tours to Cathedral Cove and surrounding sea caves, plus Slipper Island. Bespoke private charters available.

www.coadventures.co.nz
