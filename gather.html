A farm with a fifty-year plan

Hillbrook sits on 45 hectares of working regenerative farm, native forest, and coastline. The Tapapararoa Stream starts and ends on the property. Eleven hectares of new native forest have been planted since 2021. Wetlands restored. A whole-farm predator control programme running. The NZ Dotterel nests undisturbed on the beach. Guests walk through planting years side by side — 2021, 2022, 2026 — a visible timeline of slow, intentional recovery. The land is not a backdrop. It has its own life, and being here puts you inside it.
